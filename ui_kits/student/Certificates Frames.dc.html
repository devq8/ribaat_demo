Certificates — review boards

The screen is one component, StudentCertificates.dc.html, with five states. The boards are split so each file stays light enough to mount quickly and to be captured, exported and printed.

  • The empty state Six frames — light and dark, desktop / tablet / phone. What nearly every student sees in September.
  • Forty documents — desktop Light and dark at 1280px. إجازات and شهادات mixed, grouped by مستوى.
  • Forty documents — tablet and phone The same record at 834px and 390px.
  • One document The record holding a single إجازة, desktop and tablet.
  • Loading and error Both states, light and dark.

Component inventory, tokens consumed and the flagged questions are in CERTIFICATES-README.md.
